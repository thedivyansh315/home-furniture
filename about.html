Our Roots

Furniture Born From A Love For Earthy Authenticity

Home Furniture Design began with a humble commitment: in a world filled with throwaway particleboard and mass-produced synthetic plastics, we chose the slower, more honorable path of authentic solid hardwood.

Every console, dining table, lounge chair, and bed frame in our studio is born from sustainably sourced logs of oak, teak, and walnut. We allow timber to properly air-season and dry before our carpenters sculpt each curve with hand planes and traditional mortise-and-tenon joints.

The result is furniture that doesn't simply occupy a room — it breathes character, radiates tactile warmth, and grows more captivating with every passing year.

What Sets Us Apart

Our Four Craftsmanship Pillars

Uncompromising standards that guide every blueprint, chisel strike, and finishing coat.

100% Solid Hardwoods

We reject synthetic veneers and pressed MDF. Only genuine white oak, American walnut, and plantation teak are used across all our collections.

Generational Joinery

Master woodworkers utilize interlocking dovetail and tenon joinery that outlasts metal fasteners and eliminates squeaks and structural wobbles.

Non-Toxic Botanical Waxes

Hand-buffed with certified organic beeswax and natural linseed balms. 100% safe for babies, pets, and zero harmful VOC emissions into your air.

Zero-Plastic Packaging

Every shipment is protected by biodegradable honeycomb kraft wrap, natural cotton blankets, and recycled paper edge protectors.

From Forest to Living Room

The Making of an Heirloom

A transparent journey behind the meticulous stages of each furniture piece.

1

Conscious Timber Harvest

We partner exclusively with certified reforestation estates, ensuring 3 saplings planted for each mature hardwood harvested.

2

Kiln Seasoning & Design

Lumber is seasoned for 45 days to reach 8% moisture equilibrium, preventing future expansion, warping, or cracking.

3

Master Artisan Joinery

Senior carpenters shape, sculpt, and hand-assemble every frame using precision mortise-and-tenon structural joints.

4

Wax Polishing & Delivery

Each unit receives 3 coats of botanical oil polish before white-glove inside delivery directly to your room of choice.
