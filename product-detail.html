How to Care for Your Solid Wood & Upholstery

Natural timber breathes and adjusts subtly with household humidity. Follow these simple guidelines to keep your furniture lustrous for decades:

  • Dusting: Wipe weekly with a clean, dry, lint-free microfiber cloth following the direction of the wood grain.
  • Sunlight & Heat: Keep at least 1 meter away from direct high-intensity radiators, space heaters, and continuous harsh sun rays.
  • Liquid Spills: Blot immediately with a dry cloth; avoid harsh chemical degreasers or alcohol-based cleaners.
  • Re-waxing: Apply a light coat of natural beeswax or organic linseed balm once every 12 to 18 months to nourish the wood fibers.
